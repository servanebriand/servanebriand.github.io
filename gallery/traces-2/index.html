Traces-2

Binding

Carousel structure (invented by Graham Patten)

Dimensions

10.5 x 10.5 x 3.5 cm

Edition

1 of 1

Materials

hanji, botanical prints, Bugra paper, CBD bookboard, kakishibu.

The amazing and playful carousel structure is a wonderful way to explore traces left by plants and dyes on various papers. I have been exploring botanical printing on Korean and Japanese papers and was trying to get more abstract patterns reminiscent of lichens or fossilized leaves, traces left behind.
