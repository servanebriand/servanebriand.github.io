Binding

Accordion book in a case binding

Dimensions

22.5 x 13.5 x 1 cm closed

Materials

hanji, linen, botanical prints, CBD millboard

I continue my investigations in botanical printing on Korean and Japanese papers. The fabric was printed in Brittany while the paper was printed in California. Going from West to West is the inspiration for these explorations started with my botanist friend Annie a few years ago.
